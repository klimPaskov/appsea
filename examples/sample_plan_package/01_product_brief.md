# Product brief

## Goal

Help people understand and control notification behavior across desktop apps without reading notification content or bypassing operating-system controls.

## Target users

The first segment is knowledge workers and small teams that use several collaboration and productivity apps on Windows or macOS.

## Observed problem

Notification settings are split across app, account, workspace, channel, browser, device, and operating-system surfaces. People often mute broad categories because they cannot determine which layer owns an interruption. Broad muting then causes missed reminders and mentions.

## Proposed wedge

Create a desktop-first inventory that lists installed or connected apps, shows the notification control surfaces that can be detected safely, explains likely control ownership, and opens supported settings pages.

## Explicit exclusions

- reading notification message content
- intercepting private messages
- bypassing operating-system permissions
- enterprise device management
- universal mobile control
- automatic settings changes where no supported API exists

## Evidence state

The opportunity has repeated complaint evidence and several substitutes. Operating-system limits are material. Willingness to pay and permission acceptance remain untested.

# AppSea competitor research

## Competitor set

Include:

- direct products
- adjacent products
- platform-native features
- service providers
- manual workflows
- internal tools
- do-nothing behavior

## Procedure

1. Start from the user job and complaint cluster, not the proposed product name.
2. Search product directories, official docs, changelogs, pricing, support forums, reviews, and migration discussions.
3. Record positioning, buyer, price model, strengths, weaknesses, and relevant complaint categories.
4. Distinguish a missing feature from poor reliability, poor usability, bad pricing, weak integration, or organizational friction.
5. Look for recent product changes that may have fixed old complaints.
6. Record why users stay with the competitor.
7. Identify the smallest defensible wedge.
8. Add contrary evidence and commoditization risk.

Do not build a competitor table from homepage slogans alone. Do not list products without explaining overlap and weakness.

## Output

A competitor matrix linked to complaint clusters, with dated evidence, confidence, and implications for the first wedge.

When publishing a private read model, carry the research state separately from
the competitor rows. Mark fixture records as synthetic/unresolved and reviewed
seed assumptions as seed-only. Use source-backed status only when permitted
source identifiers, retained evidence identifiers, and an observation time are
present; missing competitor coverage is a gap, never a negative market claim.

# AppSea deep research

Use this skill to turn a product area or broad complaint signal into an evidence-backed opportunity report.

## Research questions

- Which exact user jobs are failing?
- Which complaints repeat across products and sources?
- How severe, frequent, and recent is the problem?
- Who experiences it and who can pay?
- What direct competitors, adjacent products, platform features, workarounds, and do-nothing options exist?
- Which competitor complaints describe the same underlying problem?
- What contrary evidence weakens the opportunity?
- Which source restrictions or missing populations limit confidence?
- What narrow first wedge can prove value?

## Procedure

1. Create a research campaign with scope, products, features, languages, regions, date range, and allowed sources.
2. Expand queries across user vocabulary, failure symptoms, competitor names, feature names, workarounds, and migration language.
3. Collect only through approved source policies.
4. Preserve evidence before interpretation.
5. Deduplicate and classify complaints.
6. Resolve products, companies, features, and substitutes.
7. Create narrow complaint clusters with supporting observation ids.
8. Research every material competitor and workaround.
9. Seek favorable competitor evidence and evidence that the problem is already solved.
10. Estimate willingness to pay from behavior and buyer context, not from complaint volume alone.
11. Score through the deterministic scoring package.
12. Write a report that separates evidence, inference, recommendation, uncertainty, and coverage gaps.

Do not use a few loud posts as market proof. Do not hide low-confidence clusters. Do not treat a large incumbent as proof that a narrow wedge cannot exist.

## Output

A complete artifact includes source coverage, evidence ledger, cluster map, competitor matrix, contrary evidence, score snapshot, product thesis, first wedge, risks, and a coding-agent package.

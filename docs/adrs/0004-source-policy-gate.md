# ADR 0004: Gate every source connector by policy

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-07-10

## Decision

A connector cannot run until a structured source policy permits its purpose, access method, retention, deletion, attribution, and display behavior.

## Reason

Public visibility does not automatically grant unrestricted collection or commercial use. Source restrictions also affect research confidence.

## Consequence

Coverage gaps remain visible and connector health includes policy state.

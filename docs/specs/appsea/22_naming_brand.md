# Naming and brand direction

## AppSea status

Use `AppSea` as the internal codename for this package.

A quick public search shows active uses of AppSea and App Sea in app development and app discovery. Formal trademark, company-name, domain, package-name, social-handle, and app-store clearance is required before public launch.

## What the name should communicate

- discovery
- evidence
- unmet demand
- software opportunities
- ranking or mapping
- a tool for builders

The name should remain readable in a CLI and MCP tool namespace.

## Naming criteria

Score candidates on:

- distinctiveness
- pronunciation
- spelling
- relevance
- domain and handle options
- trademark risk
- software package conflicts
- international meaning
- ability to support a serious B2B product
- command-line readability

## Working shortlist

These are creative directions, not cleared names.

| Candidate     | Direction                                | Concern                                            |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| AppSea        | Large field of app signals and discovery | Existing public uses and weak spelling distinction |
| GapRadar      | Detect unresolved product gaps           | Descriptive and likely crowded                     |
| BuildSignal   | Signals that guide what to build         | Broad and possibly crowded                         |
| PainAtlas     | Map recurring problems                   | “Pain” can feel negative or medical                |
| DemandHarbor  | Collect and evaluate demand              | Longer name                                        |
| NeedGraph     | Evidence and relationship graph          | Technical tone                                     |
| ProblemMap    | Direct and understandable                | Very descriptive and likely crowded                |
| SignalFoundry | Turn signals into build plans            | Less app-specific                                  |
| BuildScout    | Scout opportunities for builders         | May sound lightweight                              |
| OpportunityOS | System for opportunity discovery         | “OS” can overstate scope                           |

## Recommended interim brand

Use:

- product codename: AppSea
- descriptor: Opportunity intelligence for software builders
- machine namespace: `appsea`

Do not invest in final visual identity until clearance and early customer language research are complete.

## Brand character

- evidence-led
- direct
- technical
- calm
- useful to humans and agents
- clear about uncertainty

Avoid ocean-themed decoration that reduces the research product to a novelty. A restrained sonar or signal metaphor can work in small brand elements.

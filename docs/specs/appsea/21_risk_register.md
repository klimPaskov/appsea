# Risk register

| Risk                                                   | Likelihood    | Impact   | Early signal                                       | Mitigation                                                                | Owner             |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Broad social access is unavailable                     | High          | Critical | Connector review or contract blocked               | Source tiers, open-source wedge, licensed partners, visible gaps          | Source policy     |
| Reddit commercial approval is unavailable              | High          | High     | Approval denied or price too high                  | Do not depend on Reddit for MVP, use approved alternatives                | Product and legal |
| Meta or TikTok coverage is mistaken for general access | High          | High     | Marketing or product labels overstate coverage     | Hard coverage language, disabled connector states                         | Product           |
| Competitor app reviews lack approved access            | High          | High     | Owner API cannot read competitors                  | Licensed app-intelligence provider                                        | Partnerships      |
| Ranking rewards noise                                  | Medium        | Critical | One source dominates top results                   | Unique-author adjustment, source caps, confidence penalty, benchmark      | Research          |
| Coordinated complaints poison results                  | Medium        | High     | Duplicate or author patterns spike                 | Manipulation detection, source diversity, review gate                     | Trust             |
| Entity resolution links complaints to wrong app        | Medium        | High     | Ambiguous names or reviewer corrections rise       | Abstention, aliases, review queue, benchmark                              | Research          |
| Reports contain unsupported claims                     | Medium        | High     | Citation coverage falls                            | Evidence ledger, publication gate, verifier                               | Reports           |
| Plans become generic                                   | Medium        | High     | High human edit distance                           | Evidence-linked requirements, plan evaluation                             | Planning          |
| A source post injects instructions                     | High          | Critical | Agent output mentions source instructions or tools | Untrusted-content boundary, no tools during extraction, adversarial tests | Security          |
| Autonomous skill escalates permissions                 | Medium        | Critical | Skill proposal requests network or shell access    | Permission manifest, human review, sandbox                                | Skills            |
| Self-maintenance breaks production                     | Medium        | Critical | Canary errors or score shifts                      | Low-risk allowlist, canary, rollback, kill switch                         | Operations        |
| Model or API cost runs away                            | Medium        | High     | Cost rate anomaly                                  | Budgets, circuit breakers, staged research                                | FinOps            |
| Source terms change                                    | High          | High     | Changelog or policy monitor alert                  | Pause connector, version policy, impact review                            | Source policy     |
| Deletion duty is missed                                | Low to medium | Critical | Backlog or failed cascade                          | Deletion workflow, tombstones, audit, alert                               | Privacy           |
| Tenant data leaks                                      | Low           | Critical | Authorization anomaly                              | Defense in depth, isolation tests, strict cache keys                      | Security          |
| Public usernames create privacy harm                   | Medium        | High     | Users report exposure                              | Pseudonyms, short excerpts, links, minimization                           | Privacy           |
| Market gap already closed                              | High          | Medium   | Competitor release adds feature                    | Refresh, release-note checks, score decay                                 | Research          |
| AppSea is seen as a scraper                            | High          | High     | Platform complaints or customer confusion          | Policy-aware messaging, approved connectors, no bypasses                  | Product           |
| AppSea name conflicts with existing use                | Medium        | Medium   | Search and clearance find conflicts                | Use codename, formal clearance, naming alternatives                       | Founder           |
| Desktop app expands attack surface                     | Medium        | High     | Native permission requests grow                    | Thin shell, deny native capabilities, signed updates                      | Desktop           |
| Search quality fails at scale                          | Medium        | Medium   | Slow filters or poor relevance                     | PostgreSQL first, OpenSearch growth path, evaluation                      | Platform          |
| One-language bias distorts opportunities               | Medium        | Medium   | English dominates results                          | Language coverage, translation confidence, per-language evaluation        | Research          |
| Customer private data enters global learning           | Low           | Critical | Dataset lineage unclear                            | Opt-in only, lineage checks, privacy gate                                 | Privacy           |
| Coding agent treats assumptions as facts               | Medium        | High     | Build tasks implement unresolved choices           | Machine labels, open-question fields, plan validator                      | Planning          |
| Users over-trust one score                             | High          | High     | Decisions cite score without evidence              | Score breakdown, confidence, compare profiles, decision notes             | UX                |

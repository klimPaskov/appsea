# Product contract

AppSea discovers software opportunities from repeated user complaints.

The product must:

1. Collect evidence only through approved sources and access methods.
2. Preserve provenance, policy, timestamps, and visible coverage gaps.
3. Normalize and deduplicate observations without losing traceability.
4. Group repeated problems into narrow complaint clusters.
5. Resolve products, companies, features, competitors, substitutes, and workarounds.
6. Seek contrary evidence and weak-demand signals.
7. Rank opportunities through deterministic versioned scoring.
8. Generate a research report and implementation package for coding agents.
9. Expose a sparse leaderboard and focused opportunity pages.

Fixture data proves the pipeline. It does not prove real market demand.

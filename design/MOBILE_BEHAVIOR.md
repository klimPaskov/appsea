# Mobile behavior

The normalized mobile target is 390 × 844. Reflow the interface. Do not scale down the desktop canvas.

## Global

- Use one content column with 16 to 20 pixels of horizontal padding.
- Replace wide desktop navigation with a compact header.
- Keep touch targets at least 44 pixels high.
- Allow long routes to scroll naturally.
- Use horizontal scrolling only for compact filter chips.

## Leaderboard

Convert rows into cards. Keep rank, app name, category, score, and confidence visible. Remove secondary metrics before reducing readability. Keep the page focused on the ranking.

## Opportunity

Stack score, momentum, and confidence. Follow with a short rationale and complaint themes. Place lower-priority recommendations farther down the page.

## Complaints

Use horizontally scrollable source chips and one complaint per card. Keep source and severity close to the evidence text.

## Competitors

Use vertical competitor cards showing name, category, short weakness, and fit status.

## Plan

Stack the brief, scope, stack, and implementation steps. End with one strong primary action.

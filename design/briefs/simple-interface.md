# AppSea simple interface brief

## Screen jobs

- The public leaderboard ranks opportunities and contains no research detail.
- Opportunity routes each have one focused job: overview, evidence, competitors,
  report, or coding plan.
- The private competitors route explains competitive coverage and lets members
  inspect bounded source observations without turning the page into a dashboard.
- The private Agent access route lets an owner issue and revoke scoped,
  read-only coding-agent tokens while keeping the raw credential one-time.
- The private Usage route lets workspace members inspect exact cost totals and
  settled quota-reconciliation cases without exposing maintenance mutations.

## Visual language

Use the near-black navy surfaces, cool low-contrast borders, restrained violet/
cyan accents, and readable muted text defined in
[`design/references/REFERENCE_BRIEF.md`](../references/REFERENCE_BRIEF.md).
Prefer semantic sections, short labels, calm whitespace, and explicit text for
synthetic, unresolved, and review states. Never make color the only status
signal.

## Responsive and accessibility requirements

- Preserve readable provenance at 480, 768, 1024, and 1280 pixels.
- Keep source IDs and claims horizontally scroll-safe or naturally wrapped.
- Maintain visible focus states and keyboard order.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep fixture disclosures adjacent to synthetic records and never imply that
  missing coverage is evidence of no demand.

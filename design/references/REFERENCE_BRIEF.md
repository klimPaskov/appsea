# AppSea responsive reference brief

## Product feel

AppSea should feel focused, technical, and premium. The dark system should support long research sessions without becoming noisy.

## Shared visual rules

- Near-black navy background with a subtle radial glow.
- Deep navy surfaces and low-contrast cool borders.
- Violet, blue, and cyan accents used with restraint.
- Green, amber, and rose reserved for status.
- Strong white headings and muted blue-gray supporting text.
- Generous padding, clear alignment, and consistent radii.
- Minimal decorative art.

## Responsive intent

Desktop references show information relationships. Mobile references show the intended reflow. Share data and visual primitives, but use viewport-appropriate structures.

## Acceptance checks

- The leaderboard has no unrelated panels.
- Exact captures exist at both target viewports.
- Intermediate widths work at 1280, 1024, 768, and 480 pixels.
- Focus states remain visible.
- Text remains readable at 200 percent zoom.
- Color is never the only status signal.

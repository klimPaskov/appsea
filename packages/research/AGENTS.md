# Research instructions

Read the root `AGENTS.md` and the `appsea-research-pipeline` skill.

Keep evidence records immutable. Normalize text without erasing provenance. Clusters must point to observation IDs. Never convert missing source access into a negative market claim. Distinguish evidence, inference, and recommendation in generated artifacts.

Derived complaint analysis is deterministic and versioned. Preserve one analysis
snapshot per retained complaint, carry confidence and policy flags forward, and
leave substitutes or competitors unresolved unless explicit evidence supports
them.
